Lunes 15 · Segovia

Day trip

Segovia day trip

  • Take the AVE/Avant train from Madrid Chamartín to Segovia-Guiomar (about 30 min), then a bus or taxi into town.
  • See the Roman Aqueduct, then walk up to the Cathedral and the Alcázar.
  • Lunch is cochinillo (roast suckling pig) at Mesón de Cándido or José María.
  • Take the train back to Madrid in the evening.

Priorities

On flexible days the plan splits in two. Branch A goes deep on tapas or museums. Branch B gets a premier, baby-friendly version. Everyone meets back up for sunset and dinner.

Con el bebé · Notes

  • Madrid dinners run late (after 9 PM). Book early tables or ask for the terrace with the baby.
  • The smooth routes are Retiro, Paseo del Prado, Gran Vía, and the area around Salamanca and Serrano. La Latina, Cava Baja, and old Toledo and Segovia are cobbled and hilly, so bring a carrier.
  • The big museums are step-free, and the Thyssen is especially easy with a stroller.
  • Cocktail bars to consider: Salmón Gurú, 1862 Dry Bar, Del Diego, and the area around Sala de Despiece. Rooftops: Azotea del Círculo and Picalagartos.

De comprasGifts for the siblings

  • Calle Serrano and the Salamanca district are Madrid's designer streets. Calle Ortega y Gasset is nearby.
  • Loewe is Spain's own luxury leather house and a natural pick for a Madrid gift.
  • VAT refund: Spain refunds VAT to non-EU visitors. Ask the store for a tax-free form, then validate it with the DIVA digital form at the airport before check-in.
  • Buenos Aires comparison: leather goods and shoes there run about 30–50% below US prices. That's a rough estimate Stephen mentioned, not verified.
  • US customs: the duty-free personal exemption is $800. Declare everything you bring back, and anything over the limit may owe duty.

Weather

February in Madrid is around the 50s °F by day and near freezing at night. Pack layers and a warm coat.

Getting around

The Metro covers the city well. To get to the airport, take Metro Line 8 or the Cercanías commuter train. A taxi is easiest with luggage.

Tapas & dinner

Dinner usually starts after 9 PM. Have tapas in the early evening to bridge the gap.
